# Topological Geon Physics Engine

Welcome to the **Topological Geon Physics Engine**, a deterministic WebGL/WebGPU physics visualization that unifies Quantum Mechanics and Relativity under the Topological Geon framework.

This engine abandons the concept of zero-dimensional point particles and fundamental scalar charges. Instead, it models the universe strictly as geometric structures interacting within a continuous, hyper-elastic fluid medium.

## Core Physics & Mechanics Implementation

### The Vacuum Fluid
The vacuum is not empty space; it is programmed as an active, hyper-elastic **zero-point tensor fluid**.
- **Planck's constant ($h$)** is natively defined not as an arbitrary quantum operator, but strictly as the *kinematic action viscosity* of this fluid medium.
- Energy propagation within this fluid is mediated by transverse and longitudinal tensor waves.

### Particle Topology (Mass Mechanics)
We completely eliminate zero-dimensional point particles. Matter is simply structured, circulating light (localized geometric knots in the vacuum fluid).
- **The Electron:** Rendered as a continuous $4\pi$ Möbius double-loop. It must rotate twice (720 degrees) to return to its original state, matching spin-½ fermion geometry.
- **The Proton:** Rendered as a complex (3,2)-trefoil knot.
- **Mass:** Mass is not a fundamental property. It is strictly the geometric surface tension required to confine this circulating light. It is calculated as a strict balance between the outward centrifugal momentum of the light track and the inward crushing force of the Casimir vacuum pressure.

### Relativistic Kinematics
- **Speed of Light ($c$):** The internal wave propagation velocity along the geometric track is hard-coded strictly to $c$.
- **Inertia:** Inertial resistance emerges mechanically. When a topological geon is subjected to external acceleration, its internal track stretches helically. This physical helical stretching creates resistance to further acceleration, perfectly mimicking relativistic inertia without requiring external mathematical patches.
- **Gravity:** Gravitational attraction is driven dynamically by calculating the macroscopic geometric shadowing of the Casimir fluid pressure between distinct knot geometries. Two knots shield each other from the isotropic vacuum pressure, pushing them together.

### Electromagnetism (Gauss Shell Illusion)
- **Charge:** We do not implement fundamental scalar point charges. Effective charge is calculated by integrating the Root Mean Square ($E_{rms}$) of the internally varying radial electric vectors along the knot.
- **The Gauss Shell:** As the internal light wave circulates the topology, the transverse vectors destructively interfere along the orbit. The surviving inward radial flux mathematically projects outward, creating what classical physics misinterprets as a static inverse-square Gauss shell monopole.
