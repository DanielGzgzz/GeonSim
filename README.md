# Topological Geon Physics Engine

Welcome to the **Topological Geon Physics Engine**, a deterministic WebGL/WebGPU physics visualization that unifies Quantum Mechanics and Relativity under the Topological Geon framework.

The theoretical framework constructs a deterministic mechanical universe where zero-dimensional point particles and abstract spacetime are entirely replaced by a hyper-elastic vacuum fluid and topological knots of confined light.

If this linear wave experiences asymmetrical topological shear (such as grazing the steep gradient of a heavy nucleus), its forward momentum arrests, and the vacuum pressure violently buckles it into stable, counter-rotating loops of matter and antimatter.
